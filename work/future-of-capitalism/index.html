01 · The Brief

A competition that had to feel national

IBACC came to us with a startup competition and a room to fill. It needed to look like a national platform from the first photograph, and every part of it had to be handled by one team rather than stitched together from vendors.

02 · What We Did

One team, from drawing to doors open

We took the whole production in-house: set and stage design, build, lighting, run of show and live coverage. Our crew was on site through the build and stayed through to the final session, shooting stills and video as it happened.
