About Us

The studio behind
the stories

PTC Creatives is a creative production house based in Dhaka: part strategy consultancy, part film crew, part design studio.

Why we exist

Founded in 2025, PTC Creatives was built on a simple belief: businesses don't have a content problem, they have a story problem. From corporate AV films and national documentaries to podcast branding and full digital campaigns, we help organisations say what they actually mean, and say it beautifully.

Our visual language draws on the storytelling traditions of Bengal: the scroll, the frame, the motif, rebuilt for the screens your audience actually watches.

End-to-End, In-House

Strategy, script, shoot, edit, design and distribution under one roof. Nothing lost in handovers.

Strategy Before Camera

Every frame answers a business question. We verify the concept before full production begins.

Rooted, Not Retro

Local storytelling instincts, global production standards. Work that stands out in any feed, anywhere.
