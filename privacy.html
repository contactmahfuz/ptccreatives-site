Privacy Policy

How we handle information collected through this website.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

PTC Creatives is a creative production agency based at H-1263, Road 10, Avenue 2, Mirpur DOHS, Dhaka-1216, Bangladesh. This policy covers the website at ptccreatives.com. If you have any question about it, write to contact@ptccreatives.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us. If you fill in the contact form, we receive the name, email address, company name and message you enter. If you email or call us directly, we hold whatever you share in that conversation. We do not ask for payment details through this site.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to discuss work you have asked us about. We do not sell your information, rent it, or share it with anyone for marketing. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Cookies and advertising

We ask before we track you. When you first visit, a banner offers you the choice to accept or decline advertising cookies. Nothing is loaded until you accept, and declining does not change how the site works. Your choice is stored in your own browser so we do not have to ask again, and you can change it at any time through the link in the footer.

If you accept, we load the Meta pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (Facebook and Instagram). It tells us which pages are visited and lets us show our work to people who have been here before, or to audiences that resemble them. It records the pages you view on this site, your IP address and general browser information, and sets cookies in your browser. If you decline, none of this loads.

We do not run any other analytics software on this site.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard server access logs including IP addresses. Typefaces load from Google Fonts. If you press play on the showreel, the video is served by YouTube, which may set cookies and record the view under its own policy. If the contact form is connected, submissions are delivered through a form-handling service. If you accept advertising cookies, Meta Platforms receives the data described above and processes it under its own data policy. Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as it is useful to the working relationship, and delete it on request.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email contact@ptccreatives.com and we will respond. You can withdraw your consent to advertising cookies whenever you like through the cookie settings link in the footer, and you can block cookies in your browser settings, though some embedded content may then not work.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
